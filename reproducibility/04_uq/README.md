# 04_uq: uncertainty quantification on one dataset

This folder reproduces the uncertainty quantification study of the paper:

* Section 7.3 and panel (b) of Figure 11: the 15 most probable graphs, by
  their SHD from the point estimate and their change in the number of
  edges, with the radius of the credible ball. Panel (a), the point
  estimate, is drawn in LaTeX from the edges in
  `results/tables/posterior_edges.csv`.
* Appendix H and Figure 17: the selection of the dataset, the summary of
  the chain, and the posterior mass of the 15 most probable graphs with
  their edits relative to the point estimate.

## Setting

The ER graph with p = 10 of the comparative study (Section 7.2), which has
9 edges, with rho = 0.5, s = 1 and n = 500. The graph, its coefficients,
its error scales and its non-Gaussian nodes are those of the comparative
study. Only the errors depend on the seed. The datasets are generated with
the regime tag `"uq_demo"`. LEWIS (complexity) is run with the settings of
the paper: alpha = 0.75, d = 0.03, start at the TL estimate, 10,000
iterations with a burn-in of 2,000, 8,000 draws, a 95% credible ball, and
`center = FALSE`.

The dataset is selected by the rule of Appendix H, which was fixed in
advance. Ten datasets are generated with the seeds 1 to 10. The selected
dataset is the one whose point estimate has the SHD from the true RPDAG
closest to the median SHD of LEWIS (complexity) over the 100 datasets of
the same setting in the comparative study. The median is read from
`../02_comparison/results/comparison_cell_summary.csv` and is 2. Ties go
to the smaller seed. The rule selects seed 2.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `uq_common.R` | The setting, the dataset of a seed, the run of LEWIS (complexity) and the tables. The two scripts below source it, so they run the same computation for a given seed. |
| `choose_seed_for_demo.R` | Runs the seeds 1 to 10, applies the rule and writes `results/seed_sweep/`. |
| `uq_decision_rule_demo.R` | Runs LEWIS (complexity) on the selected dataset and writes `results/tables/`. |
| `replot_uq_figures.R` | Draws the two figures from the tables in `results/tables/`. |

## Inputs

`../02_comparison/results/comparison_cell_summary.csv`, which is in this
repository. The datasets are generated by `../common/scenario_instances.R`.

## Run order and commands

The scripts find their own folder, so they can be started from any folder.
The commands below start from the root of the repository (the folder that
contains `reproducibility/`). Each step needs one core.

| Step | Without SLURM | With SLURM | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `Rscript reproducibility/04_uq/choose_seed_for_demo.R` | `sbatch reproducibility/slurm/uq_choose_seed.slurm` | `results/seed_sweep/seed_sweep_summary.csv` and `results/seed_sweep/seed_01/` to `seed_10/` |
| 2 | `Rscript reproducibility/04_uq/uq_decision_rule_demo.R` | `sbatch reproducibility/slurm/uq_demo.slurm` | `results/tables/*.csv` |
| 3 | `Rscript reproducibility/04_uq/replot_uq_figures.R` | | `<figure folder>/uq/hasse_partition_compact.{pdf,png}` (Figure 11(b)) and `<figure folder>/uq/posterior_marginal_annotated.{pdf,png}` (Figure 17) |

Before a SLURM job, create the folder of the logs once with
`mkdir -p reproducibility/slurm/logs`. The figure folder is the value of
the environment variable `LEWIS_FIG_DIR`, or `reproducibility/figures/` if
it is not set.

Step 1 prints the spread quoted in Appendix H: the SHD from the truth
ranges from 0 to 9, the radius of the ball from 1 to 9, and the ball
contains the truth in 6 of the 10 datasets. It then prints the selected
seed. Step 2 reads the seed from `seed_sweep_summary.csv`. It stops if its
run differs from the row of that seed. It prints the numbers of
Section 7.3 and Appendix H: acceptance rate 0.0642, 32 distinct DAGs and
24 distinct RPDAGs, 8 edges in the point estimate, posterior mass 0.4276,
SHD 2 from the true RPDAG, nG_hat = {1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 10}, and radius 3.

Step 3 can be run alone. It then draws the figures from the tables of the
paper in `results/tables/`.

## Run time and hardware

Step 1 takes about two minutes and step 2 about ten seconds on one core of
a laptop. Step 3 takes a few seconds.

## Results

`results/tables/` holds the tables of the selected dataset. Step 2 writes
them in the following format:

| File | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `decision_rule_summary.csv` | One row: schedule, acceptance rate, numbers of distinct DAGs and RPDAGs, edges of the point estimate, its non-Gaussian set, the radius `eps_alpha` of the credible ball, the mass outside the ball, the SHD of the point estimate from the true RPDAG and whether the ball contains it. |
| `top_rpdag_posterior.csv` | Every distinct RPDAG with its posterior mass, its SHD from the point estimate, its number of edges and its class. |
| `posterior_uncertainty_table.csv` | The 15 most probable RPDAGs with their SHD from the point estimate, their class (Step 4), the edges added, removed or reversed, and the changes of their non-Gaussian set. |
| `posterior_edges.csv` | The edges of the point estimate with their posterior probabilities (`fit$edges`). |
| `posterior_nodes.csv` | The proportion of draws in which each node is declared non-Gaussian (`fit$nodes`). |

The tables in this repository are the tables of the paper. Their format
differs from the format above in two ways.

* `decision_rule_summary.csv`, `posterior_uncertainty_table.csv` and
  `top_rpdag_posterior.csv` have other columns than the files of step 2.
  For example, the two tables of graphs have a column `D_key` that encodes
  each graph. Their masses are rounded to four decimals. They write an
  undirected edge as `a-b` and a reversed edge as `a<->b` (a->b in the
  graph and b->a in the point estimate). `replot_uq_figures.R` reads both
  formats. They have no column for the mass outside the ball.
  `../paper_numbers.R` computes it from the draws in the Zenodo archive.
* `posterior_edges.csv` and `posterior_nodes.csv` were computed from the
  retained draws of the paper (`samples_per_dag.csv` in the Zenodo
  archive) with the definitions of `lewis()`.

Step 2 replaces these files by files in the format of the table above.

`results/seed_sweep/seed_sweep_summary.csv` has one row per seed: the
acceptance rate, the numbers of distinct DAGs and RPDAGs, the largest
mass, the radius of the ball, the SHD of the point estimate from the
truth, whether the ball contains the truth, the number of classes among
the 15 most probable graphs, whether nG_hat equals the true set, and the
distance of the SHD to the median SHD of the comparative study (2).

The tables of each seed (`results/seed_sweep/seed_01/` to `seed_10/`) and
the table of the retained draws of the selected dataset
(`results/tables/samples_per_dag.csv`) are in the Zenodo archive (see
`../ZENODO.md`). `../paper_numbers.R` reads `samples_per_dag.csv`.

## Differences after a rerun

Two graphs can have the same posterior mass. `lewis()` then ranks first the
graph that the chain visited first. The tables of the paper ranked such
graphs by their key. On the selected dataset, this changes two pairs of
ranks:

* Ranks 14 and 15 (26 draws each) swap. The graph at SHD 4 from the point
  estimate becomes rank 15, and the graph at SHD 5 becomes rank 14. After a
  rerun, the caption of Figure 11 reads "13, 15 at (4, 1)" instead of
  "13, 14 at (4, 1)", and rows 14 and 15 of Figure 17 swap.
* Ranks 19 and 20 (6 draws each) swap. They are not shown in the figures.

All masses, the point estimate, the credible ball and the other numbers
quoted in the paper stay the same.
